# AeroAcoustix — InnovAero Competition 2026

## Designing the VIP Cabin of the Future

AeroAcoustix is a project developed for the InnovAero Competition 2026 by Lufthansa Technik.

The project focuses on improving acoustic comfort in VIP aircraft cabins through acoustic analysis, modelling, noise mitigation, material optimization and aircraft integration.

---

## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to investigate the acoustic environment of a VIP aircraft cabin, identify dominant noise transmission paths, develop an acoustic model, evaluate suitable sound-absorbing and sound-insulating materials, and propose an integrated solution for improved passenger comfort.

The proposed solution considers both seated and reclined passenger conditions while accounting for aircraft design constraints.
